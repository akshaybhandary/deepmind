# Exhaustive Mode: Before vs After

## Side-by-Side Comparison

### BEFORE (Old "Exhaustive" - Actually just "Detailed+")

```
Length: ~6 pages, 3,000 words
Worker output: 800-1200 words per task
Tasks: 8-12
Max tokens: 32,000
Time: ~5 min

Structure:
├── Brief Summary (2-3 paragraphs)
├── Phase 1: Topic A
│   └── 2-3 paragraphs
├── Phase 2: Topic B  
│   └── 2-3 paragraphs
├── Phase 3: Topic C
│   └── 2-3 paragraphs
└── Key Takeaways (bullet points)

Missing:
❌ No Table of Contents
❌ No Glossary
❌ No FAQ section
❌ No Timeline/Chronology
❌ No Case Studies
❌ No Further Resources
❌ No Appendices
❌ Shallow coverage (1-2 paragraphs per concept)
❌ Few examples (1-2 per topic)
❌ No step-by-step guides for practical topics
```

## Example: "History of Quantum Mechanics"

- Phase 4: Standard Model (1940s-1970s) - overview
- Phase 5: Modern (1980s-Present) - brief summary
- Brief conclusion

- Title: "The Complete History of Quantum Mechanics: From Crisis to Revolution"
- Executive Summary (700 words)
- Table of Contents

- Revolutionary nature of the theory
- Scope: 1900-2025
- How to navigate this document

**Chapter 1: The Classical Crisis (1900-1900) [3000 words]**
- The Ultraviolet Catastrophe (detailed problem explanation)

  - The mathematics of quantization

- The Photoelectric Effect Problem
- Einstein's Bold Hypothesis
  - Biographical context
